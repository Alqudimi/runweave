# Code of Conduct

RunWeave contributors are expected to participate professionally, respectfully, and constructively. Discussions should focus on the code, the user problem, the evidence, and the safety implications of proposed changes.

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, doxxing, and publishing private information are not acceptable. Maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or restrict participation when behavior violates these expectations.

To report a conduct concern, contact the repository maintainer privately with the relevant context and links. Reports will be handled discreetly and fairly.
